# Factorio RLVR benchmark development catalog

The development manifest is generated by `fle.envd.benchmark`. Version
`0.1.0-dev` contains 36 engine-grounded tasks across five suites:

| Suite | Scope |
|---|---|
| `lab_throughput_v1` | 30 automatic production targets from mining through late-game science |
| `milestones_v1` | Research completion and rocket launch |
| `progression_v1` | Persistent early-game industrial progression |
| `robustness_v1` | Productive survival and action-restricted automation |
| `efficiency_v1` | Throughput under pollution and raw-resource budgets |

Thirty-three tasks are currently marked `ready`. Three are marked
`calibration_required`: they provision and verify successfully, but their
difficulty or budget thresholds require model and human baselines before a
frozen benchmark release.

The catalog deliberately does not claim functional train, circuit-network,
power-network, logistic-robot, combat, or repair coverage yet. Entity placement
alone is insufficient evidence that those systems work. They should become
benchmark suites only after adding their corresponding engine predicates and
procedural state-corruption operators.

## Programmatic discovery

```python
from fle.envd import benchmark_catalog, benchmark_summary

print(benchmark_summary())
for task in benchmark_catalog():
    print(task.task_id, task.suite, task.tier, task.status)
```

Every entry includes:

- a reproducible `FactorioTaskSpec`;
- suite, tier, horizon, mechanic tags, and development split;
- readiness status and calibration notes;
- a primary metric;
- verifier and provisioning metadata.

## Verifiers v1 selection

A complete ready suite can be selected without copying task IDs:

```toml
[orchestrator.train.env.taskset]
benchmark_suites = ["robustness_v1"]
benchmark_statuses = ["ready"]
```

Multiple suites may be combined. Explicit `task_specs`, `builtin_task_ids`, and
benchmark suite selections are de-duplicated by task ID.

## Release requirements

Before changing the version from development to a frozen benchmark:

1. Collect human and base-model baselines.
2. Calibrate thresholds and intervention budgets.
3. Freeze Factorio, FLE, scenario, mod, task, and action-profile versions.
4. Create held-out validation and test seeds/checkpoints.
5. Check verifier invariance across repeated deterministic executions.
6. Red-team reward hacking and privileged-information leakage.
7. Publish failure categories, including lifecycle and terminal-reason metadata.
